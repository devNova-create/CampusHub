    Quick Guide

    Marking Attendance: Navigate to the Attendance Manager. Select the target date, toggle "Present" or "Absent" for each student, and click Save Attendance at the bottom of the form.

    Entering Grades: Navigate to the Marks Manager. Locate your student, click Enter Marks, type their subject scores, and click Save Marks. The average score will update immediately.
